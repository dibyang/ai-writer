# 全书发布前复核规则

Apply `general-review.md` first. Use this file for 全书、整卷、终卷、完结、正式发布、发布前、全稿、长批量章节, or when a review must judge whether accumulated setup pays off across many chapters.
When the work has a rule-heavy setting, dual timeline, time loop, evidence lifecycle, closed-loop object, or multiple setting documents, also apply `architecture-continuity-review.md` before issuing the final release verdict.

## Core Purpose

A final publication review should catch issues that chapter-by-chapter review can miss: long-line payoff, repeated density, continuity drift, and whether the ending earns earlier promises instead of explaining them after the fact.

## 1. Full-Book Closure Rule

For every major long-line setup, check whether the final answer is earned by prior scenes, clues, choices, and costs.

Review these as a chain, not as isolated reveals:

- first hook or warning;
- repeated clue or emotional echo;
- midpoint complication or false reading;
- character cost before the answer;
- final payoff;
- residue after the payoff.

Hard blocker: if a major long-line element is solved mainly by retrospective explanation, author summary, or a late new fact, the manuscript is below the final publication line even if the chapter prose is strong.

For mystery, dual-world, historical, transmigration, or evidence-chain novels, also ask:

- Does every key object, document, wound, record, name, warning, or artifact change meaning more than once in a fair way?
- Can ordinary readers remember why it matters when it returns?
- Does the final meaning preserve earlier emotional truth, or does it cancel earlier scenes?
- Does the ending leave correct unanswered residue rather than unresolved plot debt?

## 2. Continuous Reading Fatigue Rule

Batch review must simulate a real reader reading several chapters in sequence. Do not judge each chapter only in isolation.

Check whether 3-5 consecutive chapters create fatigue through:

- repeated institutional terms, document labels, or procedure language without enough scene pressure;
- multiple similar interrogation, report, or deduction scenes in a row;
- too many abstract summaries of stakes after readers already understand them;
- emotional intensity held at the same pitch without recovery, silence, or contrast;
- mystery density so high that readers understand each sentence but lose the larger direction.

Fatigue is not automatically a blocker. It becomes a release blocker when ordinary readers can no longer say what changed, who is in danger, or which question is now driving the story.

Preferred fixes are usually small: vary scene type, move one explanation into action, add a human reaction beat, cut repeated labels, or make a chapter-ending question cleaner.

## 3. Pre-Release Consistency Rule

Before declaring a manuscript or volume formally publishable, check continuity at the book level.

Review:

- character knowledge: what each major character knows, misunderstands, hides, and learns at each stage;
- naming: person names, titles, aliases, object names, evidence labels, chapter titles, and old/new readings;
- timeline: modern dates, ancient time markers, dream-entry/waking order, case sequence, and cause-effect order;
- evidence chain: who touched, hid, moved, sealed, read, damaged, restored, or reinterpreted each object;
- rules: dream-entry limits, return costs, legal/institutional constraints, professional permissions, and what can or cannot be tested;
- theme language: repeated key phrases should evolve in meaning, not merely repeat.

Hard blocker: if a continuity drift changes who knew a fact, when evidence became available, or whether a choice was possible, block final release until corrected.

## 4. Final-Volume Emotional Conversion Rule

A final volume should not only close accounts; it must convert answers into present-tense emotional pressure. Before declaring a final volume 爆款-ready, check whether each major reveal becomes a wound, danger, or irreversible choice.

Specifically inspect:

- whether the final volume's system/procedure vocabulary overwhelms lived danger;
- whether the protagonist shows cumulative damage rather than isolated reaction tags;
- whether key allies, especially sacrificial allies, have private fear, desire, fatigue, or imperfection;
- whether every two or three chapters return to a plain human danger;
- whether chapter endings rely on cognitive summaries instead of actions, objects, silence, or changed relationships.

If these fail but structure and logic are sound, do not write `阻止发布`; write `不阻止发布，但不建议直接作为最终爆款稿`, then list the highest-yield emotional conversion fixes.

## 5. Final Chapter Irreversibility Rule

For final publication or 爆款 review, inspect the last chapter separately after full-book closure. A final chapter should not merely confirm every old answer; it must show what the truth costs in the present.

Before passing the final chapter, require:

- one central irreversible choice made on-page, not only a retrospective conclusion;
- visible aftershock on the protagonist's body, work permissions, speech, trust, or next ordinary action;
- at least one modern-line human cost when the modern line carries procedure, review, or institutional authority;
- restraint around emotional satisfaction when naming, clearing, or publishing the truth would undo a sacrifice or expose protected people;
- a final image or action that proves the protagonist has changed roles, not only learned the truth.

If the last chapter uses a sequence of acknowledgments, corrected labels, green checks, archive entries, report findings, or case-by-case vindications, verify that the sequence is broken by living pressure and not allowed to become a settlement list.

## 6. Market-Facing Score Calibration Rule

When the target is 爆款, do not inflate the score because the manuscript is complete, coherent, or clever. A third-party market score may be much lower than a strict craft score if the ending feels like system reconciliation rather than human pressure.

Use these calibration anchors:

- 90+ formal craft score can still be 70s-80s market score if readers admire the design but do not ache for people;
- high system/procedure density is a warning, not an automatic failure, but it must be offset by live cost in the same reading stretch;
- if the final review's harshest sentence is "the book is already smart enough", the next revision should not add more proof, rules, or explanation;
- for 爆款终稿, ask whether readers will say "closed beautifully" or "I know this is right, but I cannot stand it." Prefer the second.

### Gate Score Is Not Market Score

For final-publication reviews, never convert a formal gate pass into a 97+ external market score. A manuscript can pass release mechanics while still carrying blockbuster ceiling risk. Always separate:

- formal publication gate verdict;
- external cold-read market score;
- residual ceiling risk after rules are satisfied.

If the final review mainly proves completion, closure, and correctness, but cannot name the live pain, changed behavior, or reader ache, do not call the manuscript a full blockbuster final draft. Write the softer verdict: `可发布，但不建议称为满血爆款终稿`.
## 7. Final-Chapter New-Case Re-Entry Rule

If a story ends by returning the protagonist to ordinary work, the return must show changed access, changed reflex, or changed self-understanding. A new case or new body at the end should not simply restore the original professional loop.

Check whether the final ordinary action proves change:

- the protagonist cannot enter, sign, touch, or decide exactly as before;
- a permission table, role label, witness position, or colleague's intervention changes the workflow;
- the protagonist's first move toward the new dead is delayed, restrained, or rerouted by the cost of the finished case;
- the final gesture turns theme into behavior rather than a closing line.

If the ending says the protagonist has changed but the next professional action proceeds normally, downgrade final-chapter impact.

## 8. Final Release Verdict

For full-book or formal release review, include:

1. release verdict; if market-facing quality matters, separate `正式发布线` from `爆款追读线`;
2. overall score;
3. hard blockers, if any;
4. long-line closure judgment;
5. continuous-reading fatigue judgment;
6. consistency judgment;
7. only then small polishing suggestions.

If chapter scores are individually above the publication line but full-book closure, fatigue, or consistency fails, write `阻止发布` and explain the specific cross-chapter reason.
