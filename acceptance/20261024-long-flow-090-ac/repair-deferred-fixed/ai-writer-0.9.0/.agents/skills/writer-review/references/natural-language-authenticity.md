# 真人语言与自然表达审查

Apply this module to every review or polishing task involving publication-facing fiction prose, including dialogue, narration, psychological description, action, and scene transitions.

## Core Distinction

Treat these as separate questions:

1. Can readers understand the sentence?
2. Would a real person naturally say, think, or narrate it this way in the scene?

A sentence may be grammatically complete and understandable while still sounding awkward, over-designed, artificially elegant, translated, or mechanically assembled. Do not pass it merely because its meaning can be recovered. But do not equate literary wording with unnatural language: narration, metaphor, contrast, aphorism, genre stylization, and character-specific speech may be unlike everyday chat and still be fully natural in a novel.

## Four Mandatory Tests

Run all four tests. Do not replace them with a generic style judgment.

1. **当面说话测试**：遮住原句，像给熟人讲故事一样复述。Use this mainly for dialogue and direct thought. For narration, compare against the established narrative voice instead of forcing it into everyday conversation. A difference from spontaneous retelling is evidence, not an automatic fault.
2. **动宾拆分测试**：逐个检查主语、动词、宾语和修饰语是否自然搭配。Flag recoverable but strained collocations, wrong action scale, misplaced modifiers, and verbs chosen for beauty rather than the subject or object. Distinguish deliberate figurative transfer from accidental mismatch.
3. **具体落地测试**：去掉抽象总结后，检查是否还能看见谁在做什么、花了什么时间、出了什么力。Register a problem only when abstract summary replaces action, cost, or consequence that the scene needs. Do not use this test to reject a standalone summary, transition, theme sentence, or emotional conclusion merely because it is abstract.
4. **朗读改口测试**：逐句朗读。Treat two consecutive instinctive rephrasings as a lead for further inspection, not an automatic finding. Register it only when another test confirms a syntax, collocation, referent, rhythm, character-voice, or comprehension problem.

## Reliability Gate

Do not register a sentence as a `真人语言硬问题` merely because a reviewer prefers another wording.

Require all of the following:

1. At least two of the four tests expose the same concrete friction.
2. The reviewer can name the exact problem: syntax, collocation, referent, information order, character voice, scene pressure, or repeated metaphor/abstraction.
3. The problem creates an observable reading cost: ambiguity, involuntary rereading, wrong mental image, character mismatch, or a noticeable break in the scene.
4. The judgment survives the genre and narrative-voice check.

Exclude from hard findings when clear and context-appropriate:

- a deliberate metaphor or transferred verb-object pairing;
- a concise contrast, aphorism, theme sentence, or emotional conclusion;
- formal, restrained, archaic, technical, or stylized character speech;
- a character-specific catchphrase or unusual but established speech rhythm;
- an isolated sentence whose alternative is only a matter of taste.

Do not deduct from the formal score for an isolated acceptable stylistic sentence. Flag a style tendency only when similar constructions cluster, flatten character voices, interrupt reading repeatedly, or replace necessary scene work.

When exactly two independent blind-review tasks are used:

- classify the same sentence, searchable location, or clearly equivalent local pattern found by both tasks as `高置信`;
- classify a finding raised by only one task as `风格偏好观察` unless it contains an independently verifiable grammar, referent, or collocation error;
- do not deduct formal-score points for a single-vote style observation.

## Dialogue Standard

Judge dialogue against the speaker's relationship, education, profession, status, fear, desire, and immediate pressure.

Flag dialogue when:

- it sounds like narration, review notes, a theme summary, or an author conclusion;
- the speaker explains every premise and limitation more completely than a person would face to face;
- several characters share the same polished sentence pattern;
- a familiar relationship uses needlessly formal, compressed, or ceremonial wording;
- an emotional person speaks in perfectly balanced parallel clauses without a character-specific reason.

Do not force every line into casual speech. Formal, restrained, technical, archaic, or stylized dialogue may remain when it fits the character and scene. The test is character authenticity, not informality.

## Narration and Psychology Standard

Apply the same naturalness gate outside dialogue. Flag narration or psychology when:

- readable meaning is assembled through incompatible verbs, objects, or modifiers;
- an abstract slogan replaces a visible action or bodily consequence;
- a metaphor is immediately explained, doubled, or stacked with another metaphor;
- the sentence appears designed to be quotable rather than to carry the character's current perception;
- removing the elegant conclusion leaves no concrete event beneath it.

## Required Review Output

Every manuscript review must include a section titled `真人语言别扭句`.

Ask explicitly:

> 有没有哪句话意思能够看懂，却让你觉得别扭、像为了写得漂亮硬拼出来，或者不像一个人当面会这样说？

Output rules:

- Divide the section into `真人语言硬问题` and, only when useful, `风格偏好观察`.
- Quote only high-confidence hard findings in the first subsection; do not fill a quota.
- Quote the source sentence as close to verbatim as possible.
- Identify the chapter or file and provide a searchable phrase; do not invent line numbers.
- State which two or more tests exposed the problem and name the exact reading cost in one or two plain sentences.
- Separate dialogue from narration when useful.
- Keep personal taste, optional tightening, and acceptable-but-slightly-designed wording under `风格偏好观察`; do not present them as errors or deduct points for them.
- Do not rewrite the sentence unless the user has authorized revision; a brief direction is allowed.
- If there is no high-confidence hard finding, write exactly `没有` under `真人语言硬问题`.

This output is required even when typos and grammar are clean. A clean proofreading result does not prove natural human language.
